FROM CATALOG SIZE TO A TEST

More galaxies do not remove the hard part.

01 / REDSHIFT

Map the observed structure.

DESI supplies spectroscopic redshifts, classifications and quality flags. This selection uses primary spectra, GALAXY classification, ZWARN = 0, 0 < z < 2 and valid Galactic coordinates across surveys/programs. It is an inventory cut, not the DESI cosmology sample or a complete census.

02 / DISTANCE

Separate two kinds of motion.

Cosmicflows-4 supplies distance moduli, uncertainties and indicators including Tully–Fisher, Fundamental Plane and supernovae. Inferring peculiar velocities requires a consistent redshift frame, a background model, correlated calibration and a selection-aware likelihood.

03 / IDENTIFIABILITY

Test what the data can decide.

Only 126 CF4 entries lie inside the example 20° Norma cone at transformed distances of 40–100 Mpc. That is not 126 independent constraints: group membership and calibration correlate them. Compare local and external-flow models with mocks and held-out groups before claiming a detection.

Billions of imaging detections are not billions of measured distances or velocities. This release uses the measured data available to us; it does not invent missing redshifts, fill survey gaps with simulated observations, or claim that a trillion-galaxy catalog has been ingested.
