FROM AN IMAGE TO A TEST

What would change our conclusion?

The research question is whether independent distance tracers can distinguish local convergence from distant gravitational influence and calibration errors.

Evidence requirements & research roadmap ↗

To measure the flow: join galaxy identities, redshifts and independent distances; propagate uncertainties; compare predictions on held-out objects. The images above provide context, not fitted flow constraints.

To connect to Planck: the oval CMB map shows early-universe temperature fluctuations, not today's local mass. A kinetic Sunyaev–Zeldovich test would need gas physics and microwave maps with foreground, beam and noise treatment.

To study dark energy: use the population distance–redshift relation in the expansion lab. There is no direct dark-energy photograph.

THE MODEL IS PART OF THE RESULT

What this simulation assumes.

Inspect the solver ↗
01 / EXPANSION

A changing background.

A flat matter + Λ universe sets a(t). The solver evolves comoving positions x and physical peculiar velocities u. Physical positions are r = ax.

dx/dt = u/a
du/dt = g − H(a)u
da/dt = aH(a)
02 / ATTRACTION

Two prescribed sources.

Softened Plummer potentials represent excess mass above the homogeneous background. Their centres are fixed in comoving coordinates; mass and physical softening stay constant.

g = −Σ GM Δr / (|Δr|² + b²)³ᐟ²

The sources do not react to each other or to the tracers. This is not a self-consistent N-body reconstruction.

03 / NUMERICS

Deterministic trajectories.

Fourth-order Runge–Kutta uses a 0.01 Gyr step, with snapshots every 0.05 Gyr. Tests check analytic expansion, force gradients, free-particle momentum and timestep convergence.

No galaxies, initial density field, gas, dark-matter particles or Planck pixels are fitted. Parameter ranges support experiments; they are not posterior intervals.

How does the oval Planck map connect?

The familiar cleaned CMB map primarily records temperature fluctuations from about 380,000 years after the Big Bang. It is not a local mass map. Its dominant motion-induced dipole is removed during processing.

The model observer’s speed above is a simulated quantity, not a measurement of the Solar System or Local Group. No CMB alignment or Planck likelihood is computed. A future test could forecast the kinetic Sunyaev–Zeldovich signal from moving gas, with explicit gas physics, masks, beams and noise.

What would turn this into a measurement?

The next research stage needs independent galaxy distances, cross-catalog identity matching, uncertainty and selection modeling, alternative mass reconstructions, and held-out predictions. Reproducing an attractive-looking flow does not establish the real Great Attractor’s mass or its contribution to our motion.

The existing Pantheon+ inventory has 563 nearby non-calibrator light-curve records with 446 distinct literal catalog labels. Their zHD values already contain model-based source-flow corrections; they have not been used to initialize these synthetic tracers.
