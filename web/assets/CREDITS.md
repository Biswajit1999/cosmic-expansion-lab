# Observations and scientific figures

Telescope imagery comes from official ESA/Hubble, ESA/Webb, ESO and Chandra releases. It is not AI-generated. The Bullet Cluster composite also contains a clearly labeled lensing-inferred mass overlay. Pixel files are unmodified publisher JPEGs. The observation workbench preserves full frames; some images on the expansion homepage may be cropped by display framing.
